    July 09, 2026

    📣 New: Project and section descriptions

    Add a description to any project or section – the goal, the context, the thing you’d otherwise have to re-explain to whoever joins next (or to yourself, three months from now, staring at a section called “Phase 2 stuff” wondering what happened to Phase 1).

    Descriptions show as a one-line preview under the name – hover to read the full thing, click to edit. They’re reachable through our API, CLI, and MCP too, so if you’ve connected an AI agent, it already knows what the project’s about without you retyping “this project is about X” into every chat.

    Add a description to your projects →

Frequently asked questions

You’ve got questions. We’ve got answers!

Can I save my analysis results with IDA Free?

Yes, you can save the results.

Can I use IDA Free for work or freelancing?

No, IDA Free is dedicated to non-commercial use only. If you intend to use it for commercial purposes (in other words, to earn money), you should consider subscribing to one of our IDA Pro plans.

Am I getting access to the IDA development kits with IDA Free?

No, you don’t have access to IDA C++ SDK or IDAPython API with IDA Free.

To benefit from those, we encourage you to use IDA Home or IDA Pro

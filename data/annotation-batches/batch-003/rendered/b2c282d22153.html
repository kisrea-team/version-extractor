Zeplin for Mac 10.1.0

June 11, 2024

Our Summer Breeze release is here! ⛱️

Zeplin has a brand-new look — a refreshed design for a lighter and faster app experience. Plus, we've added some shiny new features:

  • A handy workspace panel to zip through your project sections

  • Updated Versions panel with previews and more distinguishable version numbers

  • A fresh Dark Mode experience (that now also works on the Web app)

  • Figma prototype previews — just drop a link to a prototype in a comment or annotation and view it in Zeplin

  • View and copy SVG content before downloading

…and a whole bunch of other surprises. Aside from features, we've also:

  • Expanded feature access across all plans, even Free (including Version Diff, Flows, Approvals, Azure DevOps integration)

  • Introduced new pay-per-project plans for a flat monthly rate, with unlimited users

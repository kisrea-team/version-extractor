    Why Waterfox

    Power user browsing without the data trade off

    Waterfox pairs a privacy first foundation with the kind of deep customization normally reserved for extensions and hidden flags. These are the highlights our community cares about most.

    • Ad blocking built into the browser, with full uBlock Origin filter list support.
    • Zero AI - no chatbots or LLMs, just local on-device translation.
    • No data collection or remote experiments - ever.
    • Private DNS, consent handling, and tracking protections enabled out of the box.
    • Tree style tabs, private containers, and UI layouts made for multitasking.

    Feature overview

    What ships with Waterfox

    Every block below pulls straight from the browser - no extensions or hidden flags required.

    Built-in ad blocker

    Block ads natively, no extension required

    A Rust-based blocking engine is built straight into the browser. Subscribe to the same filter lists you'd use with uBlock Origin - with scriptlets, custom styles, and full syntax support.

    Find out more →

    Zero AI

    A browser, not a chatbot

    No LLM sidebars, summarizers, or assistants - and nothing sending your pages to a model. The only machine learning in Waterfox is translation that runs entirely on your device.

    Find out more →

    Free Oblivious DNS

    Encrypted for privacy. Oblivious for anonymity.

    Traditional encrypted DNS hides what you're asking from intermediaries on the network, while Oblivious DNS hides who is asking from the resolver. Waterfox ships that combination by default, for free.

    Learn how we run it withFastly.

    Find out more →

    Zero telemetry. Zero analytics. Zero experiments

    A browser that doesn't watch you back

    There are no remote experiments, no "personalization" feeds, and no surprise pings - what runs on your machine is the code you installed.

    Find out more →

    Private Tabs

    Incognito for one tab, not your whole window

    Keep sensitive journeys isolated beside your regular browsing, then close the tab to wipe its local traces in one move.

    Find out more →

    Quality of life power tools

    Dozens of small upgrades, one download

    Skip the fragile extension pile: Waterfox brings tab management, status bars, layout controls, and more straight into Settings.

    Tree Vertical Tabs

    Built in vertical tabs that stay organized for you

    Waterfox ships with an opinionated take on tree style tabs — no add-on required. Every new tab inherits context, branches stay tidy, and you control exactly where the tab bar lives.

    Keep every branch in context

    Open links naturally and Waterfox builds the tree for you. Your browsing history stays readable and connected without manual tab wrangling.

    • Open links in new tabs and keep parent-child relationships intact.
    • Fork an idea by duplicating a tab and continuing from that branch.

    Tame complex sessions instantly

    When a session gets crowded, collapse what you are not using and keep only the active path expanded. You stay focused without losing structure.

    • Collapse busy branches to reduce visual noise in long sessions.
    • Close a parent tab to cleanly remove the entire related subtree.

    Power user control without add-ons

    Use Ctrl/Cmd plus arrow keys to reshape your tree quickly, then pair it with your preferred tab and session tooling for advanced workflows.

    • Promote and demote tabs in place with predictable keyboard commands.
    • Reorder branches quickly without interrupting your browsing flow.

    Match the browser to your layout

    Tree tabs are built in, integrated, and ready to fit left/right sidebar workflows without extension drift or fragile custom setups.

    • Keep vertical tabs native and stable across browser updates.
    • Choose a layout that fits ultrawide and multi-window setups.

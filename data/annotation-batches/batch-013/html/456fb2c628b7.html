Introducing Mobbin Sites

Historically, Mobbin's focus has been on featuring beautifully designed apps — both mobile and web. However, over time, we realized that our app-centric approach overlooked a significant portion of the digital design world. After all, not every great website is an app.

That's why we're excited to launch Mobbin Sites — a growing collection of public-facing, marketing-oriented webpages. Think product landing pages, agency portfolios, online shopfronts, campaign microsites, and more.

With sites, we're also introducing new filters for Sections (Hero, Features, Pricing, etc.) and Styles (Brutalist, Editorial, Minimal, etc.) to make it even easier to find the right site for your needs.

Now you can discover and get inspired by both apps and sites — all in one place.

Full access to sites is included with all paid plans.

Jul 3, 2025

Improved search experience

To improve your experience of finding content on Mobbin, we've made some subtle yet impactful updates to the search page.

Visually, the headings have been removed, and the filters now have a more subtle design. This allows the search results to take center stage, without competing for attention with Mobbin's interface.

Previously, if you started your search with a category filter, the search results would comprise apps, with no option to layer on other types of filters. This resulted in a "dead-end" experience with no way to further refine your search.

In the new experience, after starting your search with a category filter, the search results still comprise apps, but this time, it's no longer a "dead-end". You'll now see options to layer on additional screens, UI elements, or flows filters. The search results will automatically adapt to your filters and always show your expected content type.

Lastly, we've also improved the overall performance of the search page. There's no longer any delay after selecting or clearing filters, and the page will immediately start loading the new search results.

Other improvements

  • The content type tabs on the app detail page are now sticky, so you no longer need to scroll back to the top just to switch tabs.

Fixes

  • Fixed an issue where some avatars and thumbnails were not loading.

Visual search and similar screens

For the longest time, searching for content on Mobbin has been a text-based experience. Your search results are only as good as your ability to describe what you're looking for. With the introduction of visual search and similar screens*, we're changing this.

See something you like outside of Mobbin? Screenshot it and use our new visual search feature to find visually similar screens on Mobbin.

  • We've improved the visual contrast of dropdowns and popovers on Mobbin.

Copy to Figma

For many of you, your workflow involves exporting screens from Mobbin and importing them to Figma. The workflow is straightforward and simple when exporting single screens, just click "Copy" and then paste it in Figma. However, when it comes to exporting multiple screens or flows, the workflow become more tedious as you would have to first download the screens to your computer.

With the introduction of our new Figma plugin, we are now bringing the same copy-and-paste workflow to multiple screens and flows. Here's how it works:

  1. On Mobbin, after selecting multiple screens or a flow, click "Copy to Figma".

  2. Switch over to Figma.

  3. On Figma, launch the Mobbin plugin and hit "Paste".

  4. Your screens or flow will be automagically inserted onto your Figma canvas.

No more fumbling with files on your computer. Just copy and paste.

Other improvements

  • For web apps, we've split "Screens" and "Marketing Pages" into separate tabs.

  • Fixed an issue where screens or flows could not be saved to collections when viewing them from a modal.

  • Fixed an issue where multiple collections with the same name would be highlighted simultaneously when selecting a collection to save content into.

All-new search experience

Unlocking the potential of an extensive content library like Mobbin hinges on the ease of discovery. That's why we've gone back to the drawing board and reimagined Mobbin's core feature — search. And now let us introduce you to our all-new search experience.
